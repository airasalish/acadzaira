Stored answer

"20"

Solution

The maximum friction available to prevent sliding is given by:
fmax=μ×N=0.3×100=30 N
The force pulling the body down is its weight,
W=mg=2×10=20 N
Static friction is a self-adjusting force that only acts to balance the applied force, up to its maximum value. Since the weight (W = 20 N) is less than the maximum available friction (fmax=30 N), the body remains at rest. Therefore, the actual frictional force is exactly equal to the weight.
